# Krave Bk: research brief

Researched 2026-11-09. Repo: https://github.com/Novus-NYC/kravebk (untouched copy of the Novus base template). No official website exists.

## What is public

Everything below comes from the Instagram account **@krave_bk** (https://www.instagram.com/krave_bk/), read while logged out. Nothing else about the business could be found.

- **Name:** "BK Krave Catering" on the logo (black, red BK block letters, neon-style script). Posts and captions say "Krave Bk". The site uses **Krave Bk Catering**.
- **Bio (their words):** "We Cater to all your Needs.. Soul Cooking. Please give us a call @ 917-340-0332"
- **Tagline:** "Katch the Krave" (#KATCHTHEKRAVE, used on the owner's menu card and captions).
- **Phone:** 917-340-0332. This is the only contact path besides Instagram DMs.
- **What they do:** soul food catering, served in foil trays at events. Reels show trays of glazed salmon, baked mac and cheese, fried rice, collard greens, curry chicken, rice and beans, beef short ribs on a smoker, seasoned crab legs and grilled salmon plates.
- **Team:** reels show a crew in black chef coats and aprons with the Krave Bk mark, serving at an event.
- **Customer note:** a caption thanks customers for keeping them "booked and busy" with catering jobs.

## What is not public (never invent)

No email, address, service area, hours, prices, minimum order, delivery terms, owner name, reviews, or press. The site states none of these.

## Contact path decision

Krave Bk books by phone. With no email or booking system, the site does not pretend to send anything: the event form writes a text message (name, phone, date, guests, occasion, dishes, notes) and opens the visitor's messages app addressed to 917-340-0332. Nothing is sent until the visitor presses send. Call and Instagram are always one tap away.
